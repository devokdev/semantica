# Quantum Dynamics Inc. - Acquisition Agreement

**Document Date:** January 15, 2026
**Author:** Sarah Chen (Chief Legal Officer, Apex Global Corp)

## Summary of Agreement

1. **Parties Involved:**
   - **Apex Global Corp** (Acquirer), represented by CEO **David Miller**.
   - **Quantum Dynamics Inc.** (Target Company), founded by **Dr. Elena Rostova** and **Marcus Vance**.

2. **Transaction Details:**
   - Apex Global Corp agrees to acquire 100% of outstanding shares of Quantum Dynamics Inc. for **$450 Million USD**.
   - **Silicon Valley Bank** and **Goldman Sachs** will act as the escrow and financial advisory agents.

3. **Key Intellectual Property & Projects:**
   - Quantum Dynamics transfers all patents regarding the **Project Chronos** quantum computing architecture to Apex Global Corp.
   - **Dr. Elena Rostova** will assume the role of Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Apex Global Corp.
   - **Marcus Vance** will step down and serve as Senior Advisor for 12 months.

4. **Locations & Governance:**
   - Headquarters will remain in **San Francisco, California**, with a secondary research laboratory operating in **Cambridge, Massachusetts**.
   - Regulatory approvals are pending review by the **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)**.
