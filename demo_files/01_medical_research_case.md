# Case Study: AI Medical Research & Drug Repurposing Network

## Overview
CardioVascular Health Institute (CVHI) partnered with GenTech Pharma in 2025 to investigate novel therapeutic applications for existing compounds.

## Key Discoveries & People
- **Dr. Sophia Ramirez** (Head of Pharmacology at CVHI) identified that the cardiovascular drug **CardioZen** reduces neural inflammation in preclinical trials.
- **Dr. Arthur Pendelton** (Chief Medical Officer at GenTech Pharma) approved a joint Clinical Phase II study named **Project NeuroShield**.
- **BioMatrix Labs** in Zurich was contracted by GenTech Pharma to conduct molecular docking simulations on the ACE-2 receptor.

## Regulatory & Financial Backing
- The initiative received a **$12 Million grant** from the **European Medical Council (EMC)**.
- Research data is stored in compliance with **HIPAA** and **GDPR** health data standards.
