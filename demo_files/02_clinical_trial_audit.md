# Clinical Trial Audit & Supply Line Incident Report

## Incident Summary
During the third quarter of 2025, an unexpected supply disruption impacted the distribution of **CardioZen** batches produced for **Project NeuroShield**.

## Key Entities & Roles
- **BioMatrix Labs** identified impurities in Batch #902 delivered from their **Zurich** manufacturing facility.
- **Dr. Sophia Ramirez** halted patient dosing at **CVHI** to ensure participant safety.
- **Apex Logistics** (European freight partner) was audited by the **European Medical Council (EMC)** regarding temperature-controlled storage failures during transit.
- **GenTech Pharma** authorized an emergency replacement shipment overseen by **Dr. Arthur Pendelton**.

## Regulatory Review
- The **European Medical Council (EMC)** cleared **Project NeuroShield** to resume operations after receiving the audit trail and proof of compliance from **CVHI**.
